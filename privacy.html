bcms-app privacy policy

bcms-app is used only by its own developer, to manage and test their own Google Apps Script project and the Google Sheet it uses.

What it accesses

When you sign in, Google asks you to let bcms-app read your account's email address and manage your Apps Script projects, their logs, and the spreadsheets those scripts use.

What it does with that access

It only acts on your instructions, from your own computer. Credentials stay on that computer. bcms-app has no server of its own. It does not collect, store, sell or share your data with anyone, and it does not use the data for advertising.

Removing access

You can revoke bcms-app at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Open an issue on the GitHub repository that hosts this page.
